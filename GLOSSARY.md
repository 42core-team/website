# CORE events

The vocabulary for participating in and presenting a CORE competition.

## Language

**Registration**:
The initial event phase in which participants join and form teams before playing begins. Joining and roster changes also remain possible during playing until submissions close.

**Playing**:
The competition phase in which teams develop their bots and participate in matchmaking.

**Submission deadline**:
The scheduled boundary after which development, participant matchmaking, registration, roster changes, and new bets close.
_Avoid_: Event end, repo lock date

**Submissions closed**:
The phase between playing and showcase, during which repositories are locked and the tournament can be prepared.

**Showcase**:
The presentation phase in which administrators present tournament games and reveal results to participants and spectators.

**Scheduled end**:
The advertised finish time of the whole event, including its showcase.
_Avoid_: Submission deadline

**Completion**:
The administrator-declared actual finish of the event, which may differ from its scheduled end.

**Livestream intro**:
An optional introductory presentation before playing starts, without changing participant permissions.

**Reopening**:
An administrator's decision to resume playing with a new submission deadline while retaining existing tournament progress and results.

**Tournament exclusion**:
An explicit administrator decision to omit a team from tournament participation.
